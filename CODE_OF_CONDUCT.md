# Code of Conduct

Arras welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

Be kind, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and respect differing experiences and viewpoints. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.
